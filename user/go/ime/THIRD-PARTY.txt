KANADICT.BIN — third-party data notice (M186c #3180)
====================================================

The packed kana->kanji dictionary the guest reads at
/host/KANADICT.BIN is generated by tools/kanadict/pack.py from the
pinned curated subset tools/kanadict/subset.tsv. The binary lives in
this repository at image/kanadict/KANADICT.BIN and rides the share
staging like the font files do.

The subset derives from JMdict, the Japanese-Multilingual dictionary:

  Work:    JMdict — Japanese-Multilingual Dictionary
  Author:  Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group (EDRDG)
  Source:  https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project
           https://www.edrdg.org/jmdict/j_jmdict.html
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
           (CC BY-SA 4.0)
           https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

CC BY-SA 4.0 is admissible under the repository's licence rules: the
ban is GPL-family code, and this is a data artifact, not code. The
licence text ships beside the packed dictionary at
image/kanadict/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt and is carried along wherever the file
is staged.

Attribution (as the licence requires): the packed dictionary contains
reading/surface pairs taken from JMdict. Selection and ordering are the
curator's (see the coverage note in subset.tsv); no other modifications
were made to the pairs themselves.

Scope: ~150 common-word readings — deliberately NOT the full JMdict.
Missing readings produce kana-only candidates by design.
